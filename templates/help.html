Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does visa processing usually take?

Visa processing times vary by country and visa type. Tourist visas typically take 5-15 business days, while work or study visas may take 2-8 weeks. Our AI assistant can provide specific timelines for your destination and visa category.

What documents do I need for a visa application?

Required documents typically include a valid passport, completed application form, passport photos, proof of financial means, travel itinerary, and supporting documents specific to your visa type. Use our application wizard for a personalized document checklist.

Can I apply for multiple visas at the same time?

Generally, you can only hold one valid visa per country at a time. However, you may apply for visas to different countries simultaneously. Some countries may have restrictions on concurrent applications, so check specific embassy requirements.

What if my visa application is rejected?

If your visa is rejected, you'll receive a rejection letter explaining the reasons. You can typically reapply after addressing the issues mentioned. Some countries allow appeals within specific timeframes. Our experts can help you understand rejection reasons and improve your next application.

Do I need travel insurance for my visa?

Many countries require travel insurance as part of the visa application. Schengen countries, for example, require insurance with minimum €30,000 coverage. Check your destination's requirements and ensure your policy meets their specific criteria.

Can I extend my visa after arrival?

Visa extension policies vary by country and visa type. Some countries allow extensions for valid reasons (medical emergencies, flight cancellations), while others don't permit any extensions. Check extension policies before traveling and apply well before your current visa expires.
